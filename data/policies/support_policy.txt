CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY & SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA)
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

1. OVERVIEW
This document defines our customer support standards, response times, escalation paths, and available support channels.

2. SUPPORT CHANNELS
2.1 Live Chat
- Availability: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
- Average first response: Under 2 minutes
- Best for: Quick questions, order status, account access

2.2 Email Support
- Address: support@example.com
- Availability: 24/7 (responses during business hours)
- Response Time: Within 4 hours on business days; within 12 hours on weekends

2.3 Phone Support
- Number: 1-800-555-0100
- Availability: Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM EST
- Wait time: Typically under 5 minutes for Standard; under 2 minutes for Premium/Enterprise

2.4 Self-Service Portal
- URL: support.example.com
- 24/7 access to order tracking, return initiation, FAQs, and knowledge base articles

3. ACCOUNT TIERS & PRIORITY LEVELS
3.1 Standard Accounts
- Ticket Priority: Normal queue
- Response SLA: 4–8 business hours
- Phone support: Standard queue

3.2 Premium Accounts
- Ticket Priority: Elevated queue (30% faster)
- Response SLA: 2–4 business hours
- Phone support: Priority queue
- Dedicated account manager for enterprise-level purchases

3.3 Enterprise Accounts
- Ticket Priority: Immediate review
- Response SLA: 1 hour for critical issues; 2 hours for high; 4 hours for medium
- Dedicated support hotline
- Assigned Customer Success Manager
- Monthly account review calls

4. TICKET PRIORITY DEFINITIONS
4.1 Critical
- Product completely unusable or significant financial impact
- System outage affecting business operations
- Target resolution: 4 hours

4.2 High
- Core functionality impaired, significant inconvenience
- Multiple customers affected by same issue
- Target resolution: 8 hours

4.3 Medium
- Functionality degraded but workaround exists
- Billing discrepancies
- Target resolution: 24 hours

4.4 Low
- Minor issues, cosmetic problems, general enquiries
- Feature requests and suggestions
- Target resolution: 72 hours

5. ESCALATION PROCESS
5.1 Automatic Escalation
Tickets are automatically escalated if:
- No response from assigned agent within 2x the SLA timeframe
- Customer marks ticket as "urgent" after initial response
- Ticket receives a negative CSAT rating

5.2 Manual Escalation
Customers may request escalation at any time by:
- Replying to the ticket with "ESCALATE"
- Calling the support line and requesting a supervisor
- Using the "Escalate" button on the self-service portal

5.3 Escalation Levels
Level 1: Frontline Support Agent
Level 2: Senior Support Specialist (handles complex technical and billing issues)
Level 3: Support Manager (handles unresolved Level 2 issues)
Level 4: Director of Customer Experience (handles formal complaints and legal matters)

6. FIRST CONTACT RESOLUTION (FCR) COMMITMENT
We aim for a First Contact Resolution rate above 80%. If your issue is not resolved on first contact:
- You will receive a follow-up within the SLA window
- A dedicated agent will be assigned to your case
- You will receive regular status updates every 4 hours for critical issues

7. CUSTOMER SATISFACTION
7.1 Post-Resolution Survey
After every resolved ticket, you will receive a short CSAT (Customer Satisfaction) survey. Your feedback directly influences agent performance reviews and our service improvement plans.

7.2 Quality Assurance
All support interactions are subject to quality review. We monitor conversations to ensure accuracy, tone, and SLA adherence.

8. PROHIBITED USES
Support channels may not be used for:
- Abuse, harassment, or threats toward support staff
- Fraudulent claims or attempts to obtain goods deceptively
- Spam or unsolicited promotions

Violations may result in account suspension.
