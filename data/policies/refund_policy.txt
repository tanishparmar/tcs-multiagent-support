REFUND & RETURN POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Last Updated: March 1, 2024

1. OVERVIEW
We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. This policy outlines our refund and return procedures for all products purchased through our platform.

2. RETURN ELIGIBILITY
2.1 Standard Returns
- Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
- Products must be in original condition: unused, unassembled, and in original packaging.
- A valid proof of purchase (order confirmation email or receipt) is required.

2.2 Defective or Damaged Items
- If your item arrives damaged or defective, you are eligible for a full refund or free replacement within 60 days of delivery.
- Photo or video evidence of the damage must be submitted via the support portal.
- Shipping costs for returning damaged items are covered by us.

2.3 Non-Returnable Items
The following items cannot be returned:
- Digital products (software licenses, subscription keys) once activated
- Customized or personalised products
- Items marked "Final Sale" at the time of purchase
- Consumable accessories (batteries, cleaning kits) once opened

3. REFUND PROCESS
3.1 How to Initiate a Return
Step 1: Contact our support team via chat or submit a ticket at support.example.com
Step 2: Provide your order number and reason for return
Step 3: Our team will issue a prepaid return shipping label within 1 business day
Step 4: Ship the item using the provided label
Step 5: Refund is processed within 3-5 business days of receiving the item

3.2 Refund Timelines
- Credit/Debit Cards: 3–5 business days after the return is received and inspected
- PayPal: 1–2 business days
- Store Credit: Immediate upon return approval
- Bank Transfers: 5–7 business days

3.3 Partial Refunds
Partial refunds may be issued in the following cases:
- Items returned after 30 days but within 60 days: 75% refund
- Items with minor wear or missing accessories: deduction up to 25%
- Bundle orders where only part of the bundle is returned: proportional refund

4. EXCHANGES
4.1 We offer free exchanges for:
- Wrong item received
- Defective items (within 60 days)
- Size or colour variations (within 30 days, subject to stock availability)

4.2 Exchange Process
Contact support with your order number. Exchanges are typically processed within 2 business days.

5. SUBSCRIPTION REFUNDS
5.1 Annual Subscriptions: May be cancelled and refunded on a pro-rata basis within 14 days of purchase.
5.2 Monthly Subscriptions: No refund for the current billing period, but cancellation takes effect immediately.
5.3 Trial Periods: If you cancel within the trial period, no charge is made.

6. SHIPPING COSTS
- Original shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.
- For defective or wrongly shipped items, we cover all return shipping costs.
- For standard returns, a flat $5.99 return shipping fee is deducted from the refund.

7. INTERNATIONAL ORDERS
- International customers follow the same return policy.
- Customs/import duties paid on delivery are non-refundable.
- International return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility (except for defective items).

8. CONTACT
For all refund and return queries, contact our support team:
- Email: returns@example.com
- Phone: 1-800-555-0100 (Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm EST)
- Live Chat: Available 24/7 on our website
