SHIPPING POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

1. OVERVIEW
This policy describes our shipping methods, timelines, and procedures for domestic and international orders.

2. ORDER PROCESSING
- Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST on business days are processed the same day.
- Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST, on weekends, or on public holidays are processed the next business day.
- You will receive an order confirmation email immediately after placing your order.
- A shipping confirmation email with tracking information is sent once your order ships.

3. DOMESTIC SHIPPING (USA)
3.1 Standard Shipping
- Carrier: USPS, UPS Ground, or FedEx Ground
- Estimated Delivery: 5–7 business days
- Cost: $4.99 (Free on orders over $50)

3.2 Expedited Shipping
- Carrier: UPS 2-Day or FedEx 2-Day
- Estimated Delivery: 2 business days
- Cost: $12.99

3.3 Overnight Shipping
- Carrier: UPS Next Day Air or FedEx Priority Overnight
- Estimated Delivery: Next business day (if ordered before 12:00 PM EST)
- Cost: $24.99

3.4 Same-Day Delivery
- Available in select metro areas (New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Houston, Phoenix)
- Order must be placed before 11:00 AM local time
- Cost: $14.99

4. INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING
4.1 Available Countries
We currently ship to over 50 countries. Availability is shown at checkout.

4.2 International Standard Shipping
- Carrier: DHL, FedEx International, or USPS International
- Estimated Delivery: 7–21 business days (varies by destination)
- Cost: Calculated at checkout based on weight and destination

4.3 International Express Shipping
- Estimated Delivery: 3–7 business days
- Cost: Calculated at checkout

4.4 Customs & Duties
- International orders may be subject to import duties, taxes, and customs fees.
- These charges are the recipient's responsibility and are not included in our shipping costs.
- We declare the full value of the shipment on customs documents as required by law.

5. TRACKING YOUR ORDER
- A tracking number is emailed to you within 24 hours of shipment.
- Track your order at: track.example.com or directly on the carrier's website.
- Tracking updates may take 24–48 hours to appear after the label is created.

6. SHIPPING RESTRICTIONS
- We do not ship to P.O. Boxes for orders containing lithium batteries (laptops, phones).
- Certain products (e.g., large monitors) cannot be shipped to Hawaii, Alaska, or Puerto Rico via Standard Shipping — Expedited is required.
- Military APO/FPO addresses are supported via USPS Priority Mail only.

7. LOST OR STOLEN PACKAGES
7.1 If your tracking shows "Delivered" but you haven't received the package:
- Check with neighbours and building management.
- Wait 24 hours — packages are sometimes marked delivered early.
- If still missing after 24 hours, contact our support team within 7 days of the expected delivery date.

7.2 We will file a carrier claim and either reship or refund the order within 5 business days of claim resolution.

8. DAMAGED PACKAGES
- If the package appears damaged on arrival, note this with the delivery driver if present.
- Take photos of the damaged packaging before opening.
- Contact support within 48 hours of delivery with photos.
- We will arrange a replacement or refund promptly.

9. ADDRESS CORRECTIONS
- Address changes can only be made before the order ships.
- To correct an address, contact support immediately at support@example.com or call 1-800-555-0100.
- If the order has already shipped, we may be able to request an address correction with the carrier (additional fee may apply).

10. CONTACT
Shipping queries: shipping@example.com | 1-800-555-0101 (Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm EST)
